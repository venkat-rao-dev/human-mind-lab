Major Brain Regions

The brain contains many interconnected regions. Different areas are associated with different functions and work together as a network.

1. Frontal Lobe

The frontal lobe is associated with functions such as planning, decision-making, voluntary movement and aspects of language.

Learn more

The frontal lobe contains areas involved in executive functions and voluntary motor control.

2. Parietal Lobe

The parietal lobe is involved in processing information related to touch, body position and spatial awareness.

Learn more

Information from different senses can be integrated to help the brain understand spatial relationships.

3. Temporal Lobe

The temporal lobe is involved in auditory processing, memory and aspects of language.

Learn more

The temporal lobe includes areas linked to hearing and understanding speech. Nearby structures such as the hippocampus are important for forming new memories.

4. Occipital Lobe

The occipital lobe is strongly associated with visual processing.

Learn more

The occipital lobe, at the back of the brain, receives signals from the eyes and helps interpret shape, color and movement.

5. Cerebellum

The cerebellum is associated with balance, coordination and smooth, accurate movement.

Learn more

It sits at the back and bottom of the brain and helps fine-tune movements, such as walking or catching a ball.

6. Brainstem

The brainstem connects the brain to the spinal cord and helps regulate basic functions such as breathing, heart rate and alertness.

Learn more

Many signals traveling between the brain and the body pass through the brainstem.

Brain Region Summary

Brain Region Associated Functions
Frontal Lobe Planning, decision-making, voluntary movement
Parietal Lobe Touch and spatial processing
Temporal Lobe Hearing, memory and language
Occipital Lobe Visual processing
Cerebellum Balance and coordination
Brainstem Breathing, heart rate and alertness
