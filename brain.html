Major Brain Regions

The brain contains many interconnected regions. Different areas are associated with different functions and work together as a network.

1. Frontal Lobe

The frontal lobe is associated with functions such as planning, decision-making, voluntary movement and aspects of language.

Learn more

The frontal lobe contains areas involved in executive functions and voluntary motor control.

2. Parietal Lobe

The parietal lobe is involved in processing information related to touch, body position and spatial awareness.

Learn more

Information from different senses can be integrated to help the brain understand spatial relationships.

3. Temporal Lobe

The temporal lobe is involved in auditory processing, memory and aspects of language.

4. Occipital Lobe

The occipital lobe is strongly associated with visual processing.

Brain Region Summary

Brain Region Associated Functions
Frontal Lobe Planning, decision-making, voluntary movement
Parietal Lobe Touch and spatial processing
Temporal Lobe Hearing, memory and language
