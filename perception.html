What is Perception?

Perception involves interpreting information received through the senses.

The Five Senses

Sense Sense Organ Example
Vision Eyes Seeing objects
Hearing Ears Hearing sound
Smell Nose Detecting odors
Taste Tongue Detecting flavors
Touch Skin Detecting pressure
