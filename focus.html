What is Focus?

Focus refers to directing attention toward a particular task, object or activity.

Focus Factors

  • Sleep
  • Environment
  • Distractions
  • Task difficulty
  • Breaks
  • Interest and motivation

Focus Explorer

Tip 1: Remove distractions

Reducing unnecessary interruptions can help maintain attention on a task.

Tip 2: Take breaks

Short breaks can provide an opportunity to reset attention during longer tasks.

Tip 3: Set a clear goal

A clearly defined task can make it easier to direct attention.
